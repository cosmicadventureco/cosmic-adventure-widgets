Upcoming Stargazing
Events in Zion!

Worried about Stargazing in chilly weather? We'll keep you cozy with Heated Vests & Blankets!

As a certified International Dark Sky Park, Zion has some of the darkest skies in North America. These cosmic events should not be missed!

  • ✦Sept – Feb✦

    See The Rings of Saturn!

    Don't miss the chance to view Saturn's stunning rings! The best view we have until next year.

  • ✦Oct 2 – Nov 7✦

    Orionid Meteor Shower

    Debris from Halley's Comet — up to 25 fast meteors per hour. Peak viewing Oct 21 – 22.

  • ✦November 6 – 30✦

    Leonid Meteor Shower

    About 15 swift, bright meteors per hour. Peak viewing Nov 17 – 18.
